About the artist

Art across canvas, paper and walls.

Valeria Rizzo graduated from the Academy of Fine Arts in Bologna in 2010. Her work in oil painting grew from portraiture into surreal scenes. She also creates digital illustrations and has illustrated children's books.

In 2020 she joined other artists at Backyard Studios in Aalborg and started Artelier, where she offers courses, workshops and exhibitions.
